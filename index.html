About

The Sinulog Festival is an annual cultural and religious celebration held every third Sunday of January in Cebu City, honoring the Santo Niño (Child Jesus) through grand street parades, ritual dances, and vibrant festivities. It is one of the Philippines’ largest festivals, attracting millions of visitors and blending pre-colonial traditions with Catholic devotion.
